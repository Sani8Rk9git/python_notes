**NUMERIC LITERALS**



* These are of 3 types :



&#x20;1. Integer literal



* also called **int** or **integers**
* These are positive and negative whole numbers without any decimal point(fractional part)
* It must contain at least one digit
* A number with no sign is assumed to be positive
* can have + or - sign
* Commas are not allowed



&#x20;2. Floating point literals



* Also called real literals or float
* They are the numbers having the fractional part
* They are written with a decimal point dividing the integer and the fractional parts
* They can be written in 2 forms :

&#x20;              

&#x20;              1. Fractional form 

&#x20;                 -> have at least one digit with a decimal point (either before or after)

&#x20;                 -> can have + or - sign

&#x20;                 -> real literal with no sign is assumed to be positive



&#x20;              2. Exponent form

&#x20;                 -> consist of 2 parts : mantissa and exponent

&#x20;                 -> Mantissa can be integer or float and is followed by letter E or e

&#x20;                 -> the exponent must be integer (it represent the power of 10)





&#x20;                  Ex: 3.456 ---> 3456E-03





Note: If we use comma in between the int or float numbers then python will treat them as **tuple**

(tuple is a sequence of values)





type(<objectname>) can be used to check the type of the object





&#x20;3. Complex numbers



* Also known as complex
* are of the form  **a + bj** 
* a and b are floats
* j represent underroot -1 
* a is the real part of the number
* b is the imaginary part of the number
