**CREATING DICTIONARY**



* Method-1

\-> write the key:value pairs of the dictionary, separated by comma, enclosed in the {} brackets

<variable\_name> = {key1:value1 , key2:value2, ...}

\-> keys can be a mix of immutable types





* 2 ways to create empty dictionary :
1. <variable\_name> = {}

2\. <variable\_name> = dict()

\-> To add a key:value pair in the empty dictionary one at a time :

<dictionary\_name>\[key] = value

\-> if the key already exist then its value gets changed





* Method-2
1. <variable\_name> = dict(key1=value1 , key2=value2 .....)

\-> keys are passed as arguments and they are not enclosed in quotes



2\. <variable\_name> = dict({key1:value1 , key2:value2 , ...})



3\. <variable\_name> = dict(zip( (key1,key2,....) , (value1,value2,...) ))



4\. <variable\_name> = dict(\[\[key1,value1] , \[key2,value2] , ...])



5\. <variable\_name> = dict(((key1,value1) , (key2,value2), ...))
